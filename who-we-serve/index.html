The people

Five groups we are set up for

These are the situations that come through most often, and what tends to help in each.

Adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities

The heart of what we do. Respite for the family, a day programme worth getting up for, rides to it, support at home, and — where it applies — help living in a place of their own. Often the person a parent has been supporting for thirty years.

Children with developmental disabilities

Families raising a child with significant support needs, where the school day is only part of the problem and the holidays are most of it. Respite and in-home support, with the parent usually directing the arrangement.

Older adults

People who want to stay in their own house and need the mornings, the meals, the housework or the nights covered to make that safe. Very often the caregiver is an adult child who could be paid for what they are already doing.

Adults with physical disabilities

Where the barrier is the body rather than the mind: transfers, personal care, keeping a household running, and the practical help that turns a flat into somewhere workable.

Adults living with a brain injury

Support built around routine, prompting and consistency, where the same person turning up at the same time matters more than almost anything else on the care plan.

People with serious mental illness are also supported through Colorado’s home and community based programmes, and the same in-home services apply. If you are not sure where a situation fits, ask us.

Programmes and waivers

The names you will see on paperwork

Colorado runs its home and community based support through a set of programmes, each aimed at a different group. When a letter arrives with four capital letters on it, this is what they mean.

Community First Choice
A state plan benefit for attendant support at home. No waiver waiting list. More about CFC
Developmental Disabilities — DD
The waiver for adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities who need comprehensive support, including residential services.
Supported Living Services — SLS
For adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities living at home or independently, with support that does not extend to residential services.
Children’s Extensive Support — CES
For children with intellectual and developmental disabilities who have very substantial support needs.
Children with Complex Health Needs — CwCHN
For children with complex medical needs. Since 2025 it has combined two older programmes: Children’s Home and Community Based Services, and Children with Life-Limiting Illness. If a letter still names one of those, this is where it went.
Elderly, Blind and Disabled — EBD
For older adults and adults with physical disabilities or blindness who need support to stay in the community.
Community Mental Health Supports — CMHS
For adults whose need for support arises from a serious mental health condition.
Brain Injury — BI
For adults living with the effects of a brain injury.
Complementary and Integrative Health — CIH
For adults aged eighteen and over. This is the programme that used to be called the Spinal Cord Injury waiver, so older paperwork may still say SCI.

Which of our services a person can receive depends on which programme they are on and what their plan authorises, and the waiver matters as much as the service. As a Program Approved Service Agency we deliver Supported Community Connections and Non-Medical Transportation on the DD and SLS waivers, Residential Habilitation on DD, and respite on SLS and Children’s Extensive Support. We are separately licensed to deliver home care, which does not depend on a waiver at all.

Nothing on this page decides which programme anybody is on. That is settled by the Case Management Agency, or by the Community Centered Board where a developmental disability waiver is involved. If you are not sure which one applies, or a letter names a programme that no longer seems to exist, ask them — or ask us who to ring.

Where we reach

Eight counties, five CCB regions

We support people whose homes are in Denver, Adams, Arapahoe, Douglas, Jefferson, Boulder, Broomfield and Elbert counties.

Waiver services across the metro are coordinated by Community Centered Boards, and the region you are in depends on your address rather than your choice. We support individuals whose services are coordinated through Rocky Mountain Human Services, Developmental Pathways, North Metro Community Services, Imagine!, and the Developmental Disabilities Resource Center. That describes geography, not a partnership or endorsement.
