Services

Home care

Help in the house

Delivered under our Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment home care licence, for personal care and homemaker services. Usually funded by Community First Choice, a Health First Colorado benefit with no waiver waiting list, and available privately as well. A Case Management Agency assesses what is needed and authorises the hours.

Personal care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, help with eating, and moving safely around the house.

Homemaker services

Light housekeeping, laundry, cooking, the shopping and the errands that stop happening first.

Paid family caregiving

Where the person chooses a relative as their attendant, we employ them and handle the payroll and billing.
Waiver services

Support out in the world

Delivered as a Program Approved Service Agency — the designation Colorado gives an agency it has approved to deliver waiver services. These reach people on a developmental disability waiver: DD, Supported Living Services or Children’s Extensive Support, depending on the service. Eligibility and the service plan are settled by the person’s Community Centered Board, not by us.

Day programme

Supported Community Connections: classes, volunteering, a gym, people — out in the world, not in a back room.

Transportation

Non-Medical Transportation: the rides that make a week happen, and that medical transport does not cover.

Respite

Planned time off for the family caregiver, at home or out, for a few hours or a weekend.

Residential Habilitation

Individual Residential Services and Supports, including host home — help with the business of living somewhere.

Who decides what

Waiver eligibility
A Community Centered Board, after intake and assessment.
Home care hours
A Case Management Agency, after an assessment.
Who delivers it
The person and their family, once a plan exists.
