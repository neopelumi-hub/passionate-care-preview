What happens next

No sales process, because there isn’t one

  1. We come back to you

    Within one business day, by whichever means you asked for. If you would rather we did not leave a voicemail, say so in the message.

  2. A conversation, not a pitch

    What is the week like, what is not working, and which part of the system are you standing in. Often the useful answer is a phone number for somebody who is not us.

  3. Then you decide

    If we are a fit, we will explain exactly what happens next and who has to authorise it. If we are not, we will say that too.

Nothing here decides eligibility. Developmental disability waiver eligibility is determined by a Community Centered Board; In-Home Support Services and Community First Choice eligibility, hours and pay are determined by a Case Management Agency. Four questions will tell you which one you need.

However you prefer to do this

Phone if it is complicated, email if it is not, and the form if it is late at night and you would rather not talk to anybody yet.
