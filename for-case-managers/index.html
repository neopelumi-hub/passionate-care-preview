What we are, precisely

Passionate Care Services LLC is registered in Colorado as a limited liability company and operates out of Denver. It is licensed as a home care agency by the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, and a Program Approved Service Agency with the Department of Health Care Policy and Financing for the services listed opposite.

We do not publish a street address. Support is delivered where people live and spend their days, and the office is home-based.

The CDPHE licence class and number will be published here and in the footer as soon as they are confirmed. If you need them before then, ask on the phone and we will read them to you.

Referring

How a referral works here

  1. Send what you have

    Name, county, the services being considered, and how to reach the family. Please do not send clinical detail by web form — phone or a secure channel for anything protected.

  2. We come back to you

    Within two business days, with a yes, a no, or a specific question. A no is a real answer and you will get one when it is the truth.

  3. We meet the person

    On their ground, not in an office. For DD services this is where matching happens, and it is the part that decides whether the placement holds.

  4. Start, and stay in touch

    We tell you when something changes rather than waiting for a review. Cover arrangements are agreed before they are needed, not during a crisis.

What we will not do. Take a referral we cannot staff properly, promise a start date we are not confident of, or describe a service as available when it has not been confirmed. Where we are the wrong fit for somebody on your caseload, you will hear that in the first phone call.

Why this can simplify a plan

One provider across both sides of a service plan

Where an individual has both waiver services and in-home hours, splitting them across providers means two intakes, two sets of notes, two escalation paths, and a family telling the same story twice.

Because we hold both the home care licence and the PASA approval, respite, day habilitation, transport and personal care can sit with one provider. For a case manager that usually means fewer calls and one accountable contact.
