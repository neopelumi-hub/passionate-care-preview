What we are, precisely

Passionate Care Services LLC is registered in Colorado as a limited liability company and operates out of Denver. It is licensed as a home care agency by the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, and a Program Approved Service Agency with the Department of Health Care Policy and Financing for the services listed opposite. It is an enrolled Health First Colorado provider, bills Medicaid directly, accepts private pay, and is currently accepting new referrals.

We do not publish a street address. Support is delivered where people live and spend their days, and the office is home-based.

Both registrations are active. The home care licence covers personal care and homemaker services and does not extend to skilled nursing, and both can be verified on CDPHE’s Find and Compare Facilities registry rather than taken from us.

Approved services

  • Day Habilitation — Supported Community Connections DD, SLS
  • Non-Medical Transportation DD, SLS
  • Residential Habilitation — Individual Residential Services and Supports (IRSS), including host home DD
  • Respite SLS, CES

DD is the Developmental Disabilities waiver, SLS Supported Living Services, CES Children’s Extensive Support. Respite is not approved on DD; Residential Habilitation is approved only on it.

Licensed home care

On Community Centered Boards. Passionate Care is an approved vendor with all five Denver-metro Community Centered Boards: Rocky Mountain Human Services, Developmental Pathways, North Metro Community Services, Imagine!, and the Developmental Disabilities Resource Center. That is a vendor approval and nothing beyond one — not a partnership, not an endorsement, and not preferred-provider status. Which board coordinates a particular person’s services follows from where they live, not from anything we have arranged.

Referring

How a referral works here

  1. Send what you have

    Name, county, the services being considered, and how to reach the family. Please do not send clinical detail by web form — phone or a secure channel for anything protected.

  2. We come back to you

    Within one business day, with a yes, a no, or a specific question.

  3. We meet the person

    On their ground, not in an office. For DD services this is where matching happens, and it is the part that decides whether the placement holds.

  4. Start, and stay in touch

    We tell you when something changes rather than waiting for a review. Cover arrangements are agreed before they are needed, not during a crisis.

On fit. We take referrals we can staff properly, and where we are the wrong agency for somebody on your caseload you will hear it in the first phone call.

Why this can simplify a plan

One provider across both sides of a service plan

Where an individual has both waiver services and in-home hours, splitting them across providers means two intakes, two sets of notes, two escalation paths, and a family telling the same story twice.

Because we hold both the home care licence and the PASA approval, respite, day habilitation, transport and personal care can sit with one provider. For a case manager that usually means fewer calls and one accountable contact.
