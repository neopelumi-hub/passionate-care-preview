Why families stay with us

Most agencies do one of these. We do both.

A home care agency will bathe and dress your mother. A disability provider will take your son to his day programme. Very few do both, so families end up managing two intakes, two sets of paperwork and two phone numbers — and repeating the same story twice.

We are licensed for one and approved for the other. When a family’s needs shift, nobody starts over.

Family caregivers

The person doing the work can be the person on the payroll

Think of a mother in Aurora whose 27-year-old son has autism. She has cut her hours twice. She drives him everywhere, cooks what he will eat, and sits with him on the bad nights. None of that has ever been a job.

Community First Choice is the Colorado Medicaid benefit that turns it into one. Her son chooses his caregiver and directs how the work is done. She becomes our employee. We handle the payroll taxes, the workers’ compensation and the Medicaid billing.

It works the same way for a daughter caring for her father, a husband caring for his wife where the rules allow it, and a grandmother raising a grandchild with complex needs. See who can be paid and how it starts.

Who these services are for
Getting started

Two different doors. Neither one is ours.

Colorado runs disability waiver services and ordinary Medicaid home care through separate front doors, with separate people deciding. We are neither — we are who you pick afterwards.

For disability waiver services

The door is a Community Centered Board — a CCB. They do intake, decide whether someone is eligible for a waiver, and coordinate the services on it. There can be a wait, and it is a state process, not ours.

Once a waiver and a service plan exist, the family chooses who delivers each service. That is where an approved agency like this one comes in.

For Community First Choice

The door is a Case Management Agency. They assess what help someone needs at home and authorise how many hours. Community First Choice is a state plan benefit, so there is no waiver waiting list attached to it.

The assessment also settles how the benefit is delivered, and the person or their authorised representative names the agency. There are three routes, and we are one of them.

We can tell you which door you are standing at and what usually happens next, including when the answer is that we are not the right fit for you. Nobody here decides eligibility, hours, or pay.
