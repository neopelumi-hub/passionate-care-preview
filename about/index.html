About Passionate Care

Built so families stop having to start over

One agency, licensed for home care and approved for disability waiver services — a split that was never about what families need.

Get in touch Agency details
Why the agency exists

The gap is administrative. The cost of it is not.

Colorado funds home care through one set of rules and developmental disability services through another. Agencies specialise accordingly, which makes sense from where the agencies stand and none at all from where the families do.

So a household ends up with one provider for the mornings and another for the day programme. Two intakes. Two rotas that do not know about each other. Two sets of people to explain a son’s communication to. And when something changes — a hospital stay, a new diagnosis, a parent’s own health — the whole assembly has to be rebuilt.

Passionate Care was set up to hold both sides. Everything else on this site follows from that.

How we work

Four things we hold to

These are commitments about conduct, not claims about outcomes.

A straight answer, at the first call

We take on what we can staff properly, and where another agency would serve a family better we say so at the first phone call. A quiet failure six weeks in costs a family far more than an early answer does.

The same face, deliberately

Continuity is the thing people with intellectual and developmental disabilities are most often denied and most need. We build rotas around keeping the same support worker and introduce a second familiar person early, so cover is never a stranger.

The person decides what the day is for

Support workers make things possible; they do not choose them. That applies to a day programme, to a household routine, and to how somebody wants their own body handled.

Plain words, always

Every acronym gets explained the first time it appears, on this site and on the phone. If we ever tell you something you do not follow, that is our error and you should say so.

What it looks like

Three kinds of day, one agency

Help in the house first thing. Somewhere to be during the day, with other people around. And a quiet evening for the relative who would otherwise still be on duty.

Ownership

A small agency, deliberately

Passionate Care Services LLC is privately owned and Colorado-registered. Our founder does not put their name or photograph on the website. Families reach the owner by ringing the number at the top of this page.

Being small is a choice with real trade-offs. We cannot take every referral. What it buys is that the person answering the phone knows the people we support by name.

What you will not find on this site

Some deliberate omissions

Care websites are full of things that cannot be checked. We have left them out.

No testimonials

Quotes about care are unverifiable by anyone reading them, and the people who could verify ours are entitled to their privacy. Ask us for references when you speak to us instead.

No statistics

No satisfaction percentages, no families-served counters, no years-of-experience number. If we cannot show you where a figure came from, it should not be on a page.

No pay rates

Caregiver pay follows the authorised service and Colorado Medicaid rules. A number on a website would be a guess, and guesses about someone’s income are not harmless.

Ask us something difficult

What happens when a support worker leaves, how quickly we really answer, what we would do if a placement was not working. Those are the questions worth asking any agency.
